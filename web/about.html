SCB was born out of an academic necessity: global branding rankings were rewarding PR spend rather than environmental and technological substance. Here is the peer-reviewed science, empirical dataset, and leadership behind the platform.

The Underlying Research

A 4-City Comparative Investigation: Amsterdam, Berlin, Copenhagen & Istanbul

As the author of the underlying peer-reviewed study, Ricardo designed SCB's scoring architecture directly from validated empirical criteria—not marketing agency playbooks. By analyzing expert interviews and auditing municipal Instagram communications against real sensor data, the study revealed a systemic urban vulnerability:

"Branding that runs ahead of real governance and infrastructure substance functions as greenwashing that benefits short-term political actors while deceiving citizens, investors, and oversight institutions."

SCB is the only technology built specifically to bridge this gap: measuring real investments, digital infrastructure, e-governance, and environmental quality to provide an honest, defensible baseline.

Empirical Research Highlights

Dual-Method Audit: Combines high-frequency NLP text classification with continuous open sensor readings.
Anti-Greenwashing Defense: Algorithms automatically penalize promotional volume that exceeds ground-truth infrastructure milestones.
Democratic Accountability: Provides civil society and journalists with evidence-based checks on municipal budget claims.
Methodological Architecture

The 6-Dimension Audit Framework

Every city audit aggregates continuous signals across six balanced tracks.

Track 01

Environmental Sustainability

OpenAQ station sensors, PM2.5/PM10 concentrations, climate disclosures (CDP), and verified municipal greenhouse-gas reduction trajectories.

Track 02

Digital Infrastructure

Broadband Atlas gigabit availability, 5G cellular density (OpenCelliD), PeeringDB internet exchanges, and Ookla map-tile performance metrics.

Track 03

Smart Tourism & Mobility

TomTom congestion ratios and flow velocity, OpenSky flight connectivity and origin spread, and official Wikimedia digital readership interest.

Track 04

E-Governance & Transparency

CKAN open data catalog freshness, Decidim/Consul participatory democracy activity, and World Bank Worldwide Governance indicators.

Track 05

Smart Communication & AI

Conversational citizen-AI service resolution (IDA bot), grassroots civic tech ecosystems, and GDELT 2.0 global news tone and volume.

Track 06

Stakeholders & Innovation

Adzuna talent demand, OpenCorporates new business registrations, aerospace/AI research clusters (DFKI, ZARM), and FDI capital benchmarks.
