At €12,000, the Gap Report falls comfortably below German and EU public procurement thresholds (Direktvergabe / Unterschwellenvergabe), allowing municipal departments and tourism offices to commission audits quickly without protracted tender processes.

Strict GDPR & Data Sovereignty Architecture

SCB ingests exclusively aggregated, publicly accessible municipal open data and broadcast news sentiment. We do not store or process personal citizen telemetry or IP addresses. All processing runs in compliance with strict EU and German data governance regulations.

Non-Gameable, Scientifically Defensible Independence

Unlike PR agencies, SCB cannot be paid to alter audit scores. The methodology is open, peer-reviewed, and anchored in empirical metrics.
