01

The Greenwashing Trap

Global perception indices (IMD, Brand Finance) measure image without linking to real sensor or infrastructure data. Consultancies get paid to make stories sound good, leaving cities exposed to regulatory and journalistic backlash when reality doesn't match PR.

02

The Verification Moat

SCB introduces an independent scientific audit layer. By comparing public assertions against open municipal metrics (OpenAQ, TomTom, OpenSky, CKAN, GDELT), we verify whether a claim is substantiated before helping cities communicate it.

03

Earned Recognition

An independently verified claim is worth exponentially more than an unverified PR campaign. High-performing cities gain defensible, evidence-backed leverage when competing for foreign direct investment, talent, and sustainable tourism.
